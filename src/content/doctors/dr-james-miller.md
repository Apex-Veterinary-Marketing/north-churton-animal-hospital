Dr. Miller began his veterinary career as an Emergency Doctor treating emergency &amp; critically ill animals in Durham. In 1997, Dr. Miller transitioned to general practice as owner of Durham Animal Hospital in order to have a long-term relationship with his patients &amp; their owners. In 2006, he also founded North Churton Animal Hospital in Hillsborough.</p><p>In his spare time he enjoys spending time with his wife, two kids &amp; two dogs.
